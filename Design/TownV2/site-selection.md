# Site decision — 6 November 2026

Choose **Kailua-Kona**, refined to 19.63950 N, 155.99420 W for the design core.
The four 300 m comparison windows are the initial, explicitly provisional centres
from the first attempt. Raw extracts, query text and retrieval hashes are retained.
This is a composition comparison at these windows, not an exhaustive city ranking.

- **Kona:** Aliʻi Drive closely follows the curved waterfront with a small-scale
  shop strip. The palace footprint is a distinctive waterfront anchor. The initial
  window was too far into the bay; move the design origin about 142 m east and
  17 m south to bring the palace/church area into the 250 m core. Keep the initial
  extract and comparison unchanged so that the refinement is traceable.
- **Hilo:** the densest regular frontage in the comparison, but a much broader
  parallel road corridor separates it from the bay. More road space would need
  redesigning for the requested intimate plaza.
- **Haleʻiwa:** many small buildings, green space and a harbour/bridge approach,
  but the sampled waterfront is a loose edge rather than a tight civic core.
- **Sanur:** a pleasant narrow shore path and fine-grained buildings, but this
  sampled window is linear, with no obvious mapped civic anchor. It would require
  a more invented square and a westward refinement of the initial sea-heavy window.

Prefer Kona as instructed: the shoreline, civic landmark and shopfront geometry
provide the clearest starting composition. The final plaza is a labelled game
adaptation, not a claim that a public square exists at the chosen polygon. Road
width tags are absent in all four initial extracts; class widths are explicit
estimates, requiring later visual/field review, not measured widths.
