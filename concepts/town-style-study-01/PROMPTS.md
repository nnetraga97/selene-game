# Generation prompts

Tool: built-in image_gen. Four separate generation calls.

## Shared art direction

Use case: stylized-concept. Create one separate wide landscape image, approximately 16:9, for a four-image visual proof of concept of ONE original small futuristic anime town for an open-world action RPG. It must look like a polished real-time 3D anime game environment, very close to Zenless Zone Zero in DEGREE OF STYLIZATION: bold chunky designed forms, attractive clean anime characters, simplified clean textures, soft cel-informed shading, beautifully controlled color, tangible but stylized materials and rich soft light. Original architecture, characters, icons and props, no copied game locations or logos. Avoid photorealistic people, gritty realism, painterly brushwork, pixel art, miniature diorama look, heavy comic outlines, and flat low-detail geometry.
SHARED TOWN DESIGN: a compact inhabited valley town of about thirty low-rise buildings, surrounded by wooded hills and an open landscape; no metropolitan skyscrapers. Rounded rectangular cream stucco and ceramic buildings, petrol-blue metal roof edges and window frames, limited coral-red doors and awnings, charcoal structural elements, green plants. Buildings are mostly two or three stories with gently terraced streets, balconies and substantial readable fixtures. A single elevated light-rail track on chunky cream piers runs along the uphill edge, carrying a short cream-and-petrol tram with coral doors. Recurring town landmark: a modest rounded cream transit clock tower, petrol-blue circular clock face and a coral flat canopy, standing beside the small central square. Streets are lived-in and welcoming: cafes, repair shops, apartments, utility cabinets, benches, planters, vending machines. Futuristic details are integrated in everyday objects, not excessive holograms. Adult residents in original expressive anime streetwear and utility clothing, readable silhouettes, not generic photorealistic pedestrians. Distinct material separation with restrained wear, softly beveled edges, aesthetically designed props. Daylit scenes balance warm sunlight and cool blue shadows. All four scenes should feel like the SAME game, town, architecture and art direction.
No HUD, interface overlay, titles, panel borders, collage, watermark or prominent readable text. This is environment concept imagery, not a game asset sheet. One full-frame image only.
