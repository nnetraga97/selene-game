## Shared direction

Use case: stylized-concept. Create ONE beautiful wide landscape 16:9 environment concept for an original futuristic anime open-world game. User approved the small town shown in the environment references and selected style B, soft cinematic anime, shown in the character reference. Now translate that same B treatment into the ENVIRONMENT with more depth and shading. Reference 1 is strictly the rendering/style anchor: soft nuanced gradients, dimensional anime shading, warm light, cool ambient fill, elegant luminous highlights. Reference 2 is the town architecture/layout/material identity to preserve. Produce a finished polished stylized 3D anime game environment image, not a photographic place, not a flat cartoon, not painterly or heavily outlined.
Keep the town's cream rounded two/three-story buildings, petrol-blue roof edges and window frames, coral awnings and doors, central cream transit clock tower with petrol-blue clock face and coral canopy, short elevated cream-and-blue tram with coral doors, green wooded valley and rocky hills, cafes and repair shops, plants, substantial designed utility props. Maintain small town scale and inviting human scale. Strengthen dimensional shading under roof eaves and awnings, in recessed doorways, at building intersections and object contacts, and through plants; smooth carefully controlled transitions, subtle warm reflected light, good separation of sunlit and shadowed planes, richly shaded rather than uniformly bright. Keep cream surfaces cream and colorful shadow detail readable. Refined ceramic/stucco/painted metal/glass/paving material separation, restrained wear and texture detail, gentle bevel highlights. Stay close to ZZZ's degree of stylization with our own established town identity. Avoid grain, rough realism, overdone pores and weathering, excessive bloom, lens flare, crushed black or flat ambient light.
ENVIRONMENT IS THE SUBJECT: architecture and playable space dominate. Only a few SMALL adult anime townspeople in the middle or far distance for scale, rendered consistently with style B. No large foreground characters or portrait composition. Keep near and mid-distance architecture sharp and readable; only distant hills get atmospheric softness. No HUD, typography, labels, watermarks, split panels or collage. One full-frame image.
