# Character reference study — 02

Two original adult women in the approved style B, following the wardrobe direction (more skin, more interesting clothes; adult and tasteful). Framed at eye level in a near-A-pose for 3D use. **Approved by the user (November 5).** Not yet assigned as main characters or NPCs.

## 01 — Festival champion and skyrail racer

![Festival champion and skyrail racer](01-festival-champion.png)

Original design. Inspiration was limited to personality and energy: the graceful, warm champion energy of Pyrrha Nikos (RWBY). Their signature looks are deliberately excluded (circlet, bronze breastplate, red sash, high ponytail, spear and shield). No existing character's look, name or outfit was copied.

## 02 — Night-market fixer and rooftop courier

![Night-market fixer and rooftop courier](02-night-market-fixer.png)

Original design. Inspiration was limited to personality and energy: the sharp, sleek, dangerous energy of Katarina (League of Legends). Their signature looks are deliberately excluded (long straight red hair, eye scar, daggers, red-and-black assassin outfit). No existing character's look, name or outfit was copied.
