# Eclipse Warden: Tripo text-to-image prompt pack (v2)

Draft, 2026-11-13. Not yet approved. v2 changes: light red hair, no circlet, no scar, and the Selene style B rendering block in every prompt.

## How to get Selene's style through

1. **Attach a style reference image** wherever Tripo allows one. Use `concepts/character-reference-study-02/01-festival-champion.png`, which is approved style B: a full body on a plain backdrop, close to these prompts' framing. The text alone won't hold the style; the reference image does most of the work.
2. Every prompt opens with the same **STYLE B block**. It's taken from the approved style study (`concepts/character-style-study-01/PROMPTS.md`, variant B) and the approved character sheets (study 02). Don't trim it.
3. Generate **1 (front)** first. Once it's approved, attach it too as the identity reference for every later prompt.
4. Her left and right are always HER left and right. The shield, crescent pauldron and full arm plate are on her left; the sword and single bracer are on her right.

Locked colours: ivory #EDE6D6 · gold #C9A24A · crimson #9E1B24 · dark leather #3B2A22 · hair light red #E07B6E · glow red-orange #FF5A2A (only on the sternum ring, sword edge and shield ring).

Tripo's **Remove Lighting** option must be on for the 3D step: style B images carry soft shading that shouldn't end up baked into the texture.
