# FreeStylized license research

Source: https://freestylized.com/disclaimer/ — checked 2026-11-06.

No FreeStylized assets are present in this directory yet.

The site's License section permits free commercial and non-commercial use
without separate permission. Attribution is appreciated but is not required.
It describes its license as a custom CC0 license, but adds redistribution
restrictions, so it must not be represented as unrestricted standard CC0.

The added terms prohibit redistribution attributed to someone other than
FreeStylized or the content authors, and prohibit redistribution on other
platforms unless the content is substantially modified and repurposed, such as
incorporation into a model, kit, or asset pack. Patreon products have separate
terms and were not considered for this task.

For a future acquisition, record each asset's source page, direct archive URL,
hash, resolution, and any asset-specific terms before import. Keep original
downloaded images out of Git and retain this provenance with the project.
